About Me

Hello,

My name is Aleksey — I’m a system administrator at the international company Euro Food Service↗ and a graduate of Kharkiv National University of Radio Electronics, holding a Master’s degree in Computer Engineering.

In my work, I am responsible for the stable operation and development of the company’s IT infrastructure. My main focus areas include access control systems (ACS), IP telephony, video surveillance, and office equipment. I ensure the proper operation of thin clients, thermal printers, and data collection terminals (DCT), as well as integrating devices across various software platforms — Windows, Android, and Linux. I also monitor system performance and resolve technical issues. My goal is to ensure all IT systems operate stably, seamlessly for users, and as efficiently as possible for the business.

Alongside my main work, I am deeply involved in web development, particularly in the frontend field, focusing on creating web applications and layouts. I have a solid theoretical and practical background in HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, along with several related technologies that enable me to build adaptive, fast, and functional web interfaces effectively.

My projects include developing various web resources such as landing pages and multipage websites of different complexity levels. One of them is an interactive web application for assessing physical condition — a smart calculator.

This website is the result of my learning experiments with modern technologies and development approaches. It reflects my professional journey, showcasing not only technical skills but also attention to structure, design, and user experience.

I combine the technical discipline of a system administrator with the attention to detail and structured thinking of a web developer. I am open to new projects both in the field of computer maintenance and configuration, as well as in web development — from creating landing pages to full-fledged websites.
